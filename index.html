Your CCTV already knows what happened in your shop.Why are you still the one who has to watch it?

  • You can't be there all day

    The shop is open all day, but you can't be physically present for all of it.

  • The answers are buried in footage

    Today you either let it go, or sit with your DVR (the box that records your CCTV) at night, scrubbing through hours of footage.

  • You find out after the money is gone

    Missed customers, understaffing, late opening, long queues and stock-outs come to light only after the sale is already lost.

How ShopLens works

No new cameras. ShopLens uses the CCTV you already have.

  1. Connect your existing CCTV

    ShopLens connects to your CCTV and the DVR that records it.

  2. It watches for what costs you money

    Not everything. Only a small set of high-value events that can be clearly seen on camera.

  3. You get WhatsApp alerts and a daily summary

    A short message when money is at risk. And if nothing unusual happened, you still get your daily summary.

What ShopLens alerts you about

Missed customers and understaffing

Customers walking in when there aren't enough staff at the counter.

Example alert8 customers entered between 5:10–5:35 pm while only one staff member was visible at the counter. 3 left within 2 minutes.

Stock-outs

A shelf that appears empty for too long.

Example alertYour biscuit shelf appears empty for 47 minutes today. Check stock before tomorrow's evening rush.

Late opening

The shop opening later than your usual time.

Example alertNo customer-facing activity detected until 9:24 am. Your usual opening time is 9:00 am.

Long queues

Customers left waiting in a long queue.

What ShopLens sends you, and what it doesn't

Built to protect your money, not to watch your people.

  • Summaries, not footage

    You get a short money-at-risk summary on WhatsApp, not video clips.

  • Not employee surveillance

    ShopLens looks for events that cost you money, not at what each person is doing.

  • Honest when unsure

    If ShopLens isn't certain, it labels the alert possible. It never presents a guess as fact.
